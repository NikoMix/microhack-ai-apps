# Zava Smart-Garment Warranty

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01 · **Owner:** Zava Product Quality

## Coverage
- **2-year limited warranty** on embedded electronics (sensors, battery, Bluetooth module) from the
  date of purchase.
- **1-year warranty** on fabric integrity (seams, sensor-fabric bonding) under normal use.
- Warranty covers **manufacturing defects**, not accidental damage, misuse, or wash-care violations.

## Battery
- Rechargeable modules are warranted to retain **≥80% capacity for 500 charge cycles**.
- Batteries are user-replaceable on jackets and trainers; sealed on socks/headbands.

## What voids the warranty
- Machine washing above 40°C, tumble drying, ironing over sensor zones, or bleaching.
- Opening sealed sensor pods or using non-Zava chargers.

## Claims
1. In the Zava app: Support → **Warranty claim**; attach order ID and a short fault description.
2. Run the in-app **self-diagnostic** (Device → Diagnostics) and attach the report.
3. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within **10 business days**.
