# Zava Returns & Exchange Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01 · **Owner:** Zava Customer Care

## Standard returns
- Items may be returned within **45 days** of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Items must be in **resellable condition** with tags attached. Smart garments must be **factory reset**
  in the Zava app (Settings → Device → Erase data) before return.
- Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** of receipt at the Zava warehouse.

## Smart-garment specifics
- Electronics-embedded items (sensors, batteries) carry a **14-day "no questions" window** for
  change-of-mind, and the full 45-day window for defects.
- A return is **free** if the item is **defective** or the wrong item was shipped.
- Change-of-mind returns of smart garments incur a **$7.95** restocking/sanitisation fee.

## Exchanges
- Size and color exchanges are free within 45 days, subject to stock.
- For connected footwear (e.g., Glide Connected Trainers), the insole IMU must be unpaired first.

## Non-returnable
- Smart socks and headbands worn against skin are non-returnable once the hygiene seal is broken,
  **unless defective**.

## How to start a return
1. Open the Zava app or web account → Orders → select order → **Start a return**.
2. Choose reason; print the prepaid label (defective/wrong-item) or pay the restocking fee.
3. Drop at any carrier point within 14 days of label creation.

> Wholesale (B2B) orders follow the contract terms in the Master Services Agreement, not this policy.
