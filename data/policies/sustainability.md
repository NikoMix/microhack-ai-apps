# Zava Sustainability Commitments

**Owner:** Zava ESG Office · **Reporting year:** 2026

## Materials
- **70%+ recycled or bio-based fibers** across the lifestyle range by end of 2026.
- Sensor yarns use **lead-free, RoHS-compliant** conductive coatings.
- Packaging is **plastic-free** and curbside-recyclable.

## Circularity
- **Zava ReWear:** trade in any working smart garment for store credit; we refurbish or harvest sensors.
- **Battery take-back:** free mail-in recycling for all Zava battery modules.
- Target: **zero e-textile waste to landfill** from returns by 2027.

## Carbon
- Carbon-neutral shipping on all consumer orders (verified offsets).
- Manufacturing partners audited annually against the **Zava Supplier Code of Conduct**.

## Repairability
- 2-year electronics warranty and **user-replaceable batteries** on jackets, trainers, and gloves.
- Published repair guides extend product life and reduce waste.

## Transparency
- Annual ESG report published each Q1; methodology available on request.
