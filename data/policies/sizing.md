# Zava Sizing & Fit Guide

**Owner:** Zava Product · For best sensor accuracy, smart garments should fit **snug, not tight**.

## Tops (shirts, bras, tees)
| Size | Chest (in) | Chest (cm) |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 30–32 | 76–81 |
| S | 33–35 | 84–89 |
| M | 36–38 | 91–97 |
| L | 39–41 | 99–104 |
| XL | 42–44 | 107–112 |
| XXL | 45–47 | 114–119 |

## Bottoms (leggings, shorts)
| Size | Waist (in) | Hip (in) |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 24–26 | 33–35 |
| S | 27–29 | 36–38 |
| M | 30–32 | 39–41 |
| L | 33–35 | 42–44 |
| XL | 36–38 | 45–47 |

## Connected footwear (Glide Trainers)
- Runs **true to size**; for wide feet, size up half a size.
- The insole IMU calibrates to your stride during the first 2 km walk.

## Sensor-fit tips
- Heart-rate fabric needs skin contact across the chest band area — avoid loose layering underneath.
- Compression leggings should leave **no gap** at the knee thermistor zone.
- If readings look noisy, re-seat the garment and re-run **Device → Calibrate** in the app.
