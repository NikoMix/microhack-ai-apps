# Caring for Your Zava Smart Garments

Proper care keeps sensors accurate and protects your warranty.

## Before washing
1. **Remove the battery module** where applicable (jackets, trainers insole, gloves).
2. Close all sensor-pod covers and fasten hook-and-loop tabs.
3. Use the **Zava wash bag** for socks, headbands, and bras.

## Washing
- **Cold or warm wash, max 40°C**, gentle/delicate cycle.
- Use mild detergent. **No bleach, no fabric softener** (softener coats sensors and degrades signal).
- Turn garments inside out to protect printed sensor traces.

## Drying
- **Air-dry flat**, away from direct heat and sunlight.
- **Never tumble dry** smart garments — heat damages the sensor-fabric bond.

## Don't
- Don't iron over sensor zones or printed traces.
- Don't dry-clean.
- Don't fully submerge battery modules.

## Recharging
- Use only the supplied **Zava magnetic charger**.
- A full charge takes ~90 minutes; the LED turns green when complete.

## Troubleshooting accuracy
- Noisy data after many washes? Run **Device → Re-calibrate**.
- Persistent dropouts may indicate end-of-life sensor fabric (see warranty for the 500-cycle spec).
